PILOT'S MANUAL

How to play

  • Daily Flight: ten fixed prompts everyone plays on the same UTC date. Unlimited: ten practice prompts in your chosen category — movies, video games, history, and more.
  • Daily progress and streaks are saved separately from unlimited flights, so practice never consumes your daily attempt.
  • Common spelling mistakes get local answer-bank suggestions. Choose the intended answer to get its full score.
  • Type any answer that belongs in the category before 25 seconds run out.
  • Cloud Daily scores use verified past-player rarity after enough submissions, with editorial tiers as a fallback. Guest and practice scores use editorial tiers.
  • Your score is rocket fuel: rare answers fly much farther and can pass multiple planets in one jump. Each planet requires more cumulative points to reach. Incorrect, unanswered and passed prompts add no distance.
  • The timer pauses while the rules are open.
  • Unlisted cloud answers can be checked by an optional AI reviewer or reported for human review. Unverified answers never inflate the public leaderboard. Guest / practice runs can self-confirm unlisted answers for 10 points.
  • There are eight official planets; Ceres and Pluto are dwarf planets. Beyond Pluto are the Kuiper Belt, theoretical Oort Cloud, Alpha Centauri and a fictional VOIDRIFT.
  • Orbital order and AU numbers are based on real astronomy; planet display sizes and flight times are cinematic, not physically scaled.
